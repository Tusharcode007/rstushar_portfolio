How I write a bug report

  1. Environment — browser, device, OS or Companion build.
  2. Numbered repro steps — anyone can follow them cold.
  3. Expected vs. actual — stated separately, no guessing.
  4. Evidence — annotated screenshots or the exact source line.
  5. Impact — who it hurts and why it's worth fixing.
